Cadence helps you practise regularly without having to keep it in your head: for an instrument, a talk, an exam or anything else that takes practice.

You set a goal, for example 100 minutes of piano a week or two hours before Thursday's talk at 10 am. Then you add when you actually have time, say Thursday to Sunday from 5 to 6 pm. Cadence spreads the goal over those times and shows you each day how much is due today.

The plan adapts. Practise more one day and the next days get lighter, sometimes a day becomes completely free. Miss a day and the rest is spread out again instead of piling up. A weekly goal can also start its week at a lesson: practice before Monday's piano lesson still counts for the week before.

Reminders arrive when your free time starts and there's still something to do today. At most two or three a day, never once you're done and never right after practising. Ignore them for a few days and they get quieter. If you're ahead, Cadence tells you that today is off.

Also included: a timer that keeps running when the app is closed, with progress in its notification, several goals side by side, a history with calendar and weekly overview, a streak that doesn't break on your days off, a light and dark home screen widget, notes and CSV export.

German and English. No account, no ads, no trackers, no server. If Android backup is on, it may include the app data.
